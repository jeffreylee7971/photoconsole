## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-04-03 | Denial of Service | run_consolidation: large library exhausts disk at destination | accept | Preflight check validates writable; space check deferred (user reviews dry-run plan first per D-16) |
